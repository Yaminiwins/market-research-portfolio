My approach

My work starts with a business question. I define the market and scope, identify credible evidence, validate assumptions, analyse the competitive and industry landscape, and translate the findings into an executive-ready narrative.

I have worked across ICT, Chemicals, Semiconductors, Electronics, Consumer Goods, Industrial Automation, Food & Beverage, Pharma and other emerging markets.
